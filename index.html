About the cabin

Restored by hand, hosted with heart

We love giving our guests a great time in the mountains, and this cabin is where we put that love to work. We have spent countless hours bringing it back to life ourselves, keeping the warm wood walls and beams that give it character while making every room comfortable for today.

  • A hand-whittled mantelThe log mantel in the living room was whittled by hand, one shaving at a time.
  • Cabinets brought back to lifeWe sanded and completely restored the kitchen cabinets from their original state, then paired them with new quartz countertops.
  • The little thingsA stocked coffee bar, a guidebook full of our favorite local spots, and a host who is only a message away.

We hope it feels like your place in the mountains.
— Greg
